# Where do calendar events live?
Decided in #10

**Applies when:** showing, creating, moving or storing events.

**Answer:** In Google Calendar, which stays the source of truth. Foyer shows events as stickers and can create and move them, writing every change back to Google. It doesn't keep a calendar of its own.

**Why:** Google Calendar is already complete, and people keep using it outside Foyer. Foyer connects to good existing tools rather than rebuilding them.

**Rejected:** A calendar owned by Foyer, which would mean rebuilding a mature product and keeping two calendars in step.
