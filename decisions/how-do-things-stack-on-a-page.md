# How do things stack on a page?
Decided in #10

**Applies when:** drawing a page, or adding, moving or erasing anything on it.

**Answer:** In the order they were added, as on paper: a scribble made after a sticker sits on top of it, and a sticker added after a scribble sits on top of the scribble. Moving something brings it to the top, like peeling it off and sticking it down again. The eraser only removes ink.

Until ink option C (milestone 9), ink is drawn above stickers and text.

**Why:** The messy, layered look is part of what makes Foyer feel tangible and fun.
