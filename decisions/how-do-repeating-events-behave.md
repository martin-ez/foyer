# How do repeating events behave?
Decided in #10

**Applies when:** showing, moving or editing an event that repeats in Google Calendar.

**Answer:** Every occurrence is its own sticker on its own day. Moving a sticker changes only that occurrence. Changing the whole series happens in Google Calendar.

**Why:** Google Calendar already handles repetition, so Foyer only has to show it. Moving one occurrence at a time keeps the board simple and avoids rebuilding Google's "this event or all following?" choice.

**Rejected:** Editing whole series from Foyer, which would rebuild part of Google Calendar.
