# What does a sticker's position mean?
Decided in #10

**Applies when:** placing, moving or laying out stickers on any page.

**Answer:**
- **Calendar pages:** position means date. An item appears on its day, and sticking an item on a day gives it that date. Moving a sticker to another day asks "Did you mean to change the date?"; declining puts it back.
- **Day page:** height also means time, with snapping. Moving a sticker up or down changes its time without asking. Items with a date but no time sit apart from the timeline.
- **Month and week pages:** position within a day has no time meaning.
- **Free pages** (notebook and body pages): position means nothing; a sticker is a reference placed by hand.

Each sticker has a fixed spot on its page, the same for everyone. New stickers take the next free spot and can be moved within their day.

**Why:** Moving a sticker should feel like rescheduling on paper. Fixed spots keep the page identical for everyone, so shared scribbles stay next to the sticker they're about.

**Rejected:** Stickers arranged separately for each person, where shared scribbles can end up next to the wrong sticker.
