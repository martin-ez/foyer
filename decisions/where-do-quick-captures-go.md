# Where do quick captures go?
Decided in #10

**Applies when:** capturing anything quickly, from any device or shortcut, or sorting captured items.

**Answer:** Quick capture always creates a task, in the capturer's private Inbox, where only they can see it. Later it's sorted into a space and given a date, or turned into another kind of item (a note or an event), keeping what the two share: title, description and page. Tasks don't need a date.

**Why:** Capture has to be as fast as Todoist's, so it can't ask what kind of item something is. Sorting later, as in Getting Things Done, keeps capture instant, and keeping the Inbox private lets quick thoughts stay personal until filed.

**Rejected:** Capturing plain text and deciding its type later, which adds a decision to every item.
