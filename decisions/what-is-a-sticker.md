# What is a sticker?
Decided in #10

**Applies when:** showing an item on a page, or deciding what tapping or ticking something on a page does.

**Answer:** A link to an item (a task, event or note) placed on a page. The item itself lives in its space, and the same item can appear as stickers on several pages. A task sticker shows its title and a checkbox you can tick right on the page; a note sticker shows a thumbnail of the note. Tapping a sticker opens the item's full view. Decorative stickers, such as images or emoji, link to nothing.

**Why:** One object shown in many places, with its source of truth elsewhere, keeps every view consistent: ticking a task anywhere ticks it everywhere.
