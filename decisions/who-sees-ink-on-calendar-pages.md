# Who sees ink on calendar pages?
Decided in #10

**Applies when:** drawing, syncing or showing ink on month, week or day pages.

**Answer:** The whole household. Scribbles on calendar pages are always shared, even though the stickers on them keep the visibility of their own space. Private handwriting belongs in a personal notebook or an item's body page.

**Why:** The calendar is the household's shared board, like one on the fridge. Keeping its ink in a single shared layer avoids the scope of separate boards or layers.

**Rejected:** A separate board per person or space, which means hopping between boards. Stacked transparent layers per space, which is more tangible but more scope.
