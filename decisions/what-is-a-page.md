# What is a page?
Decided in #10

**Applies when:** building any view people write on, or deciding how something is laid out or extended.

**Answer:** A paper-like sheet with a fixed shape, and the unit everything in Foyer happens on: calendar pages (month, week and day), notebook pages and items' body pages. A page never stretches, and it looks the same on every device, so ink and stickers land in the same place everywhere.

**Why:** Foyer should feel tangible, like a planner and a notebook. A fixed shape is what keeps scribbles in the same place on every device and at every zoom level.

**Rejected:** An infinite canvas, where people get lost. Pages that grow to fit their content, which would move ink out of place.
