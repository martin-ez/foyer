# How do you move around the calendar?
Decided in #10

**Applies when:** designing navigation and gestures on calendar pages.

**Answer:** Switching between month, week and day has its own clear control, following Google Calendar's familiar patterns. Pinch zooms within the current view, so people can write in detail; it doesn't switch between views.

**Why:** Writing on pages is central, so pinch belongs to zooming in to write. Navigation that relies on pinch alone is hard to discover.

**Rejected:** Pinching to move between month, week and day, which makes the gesture ambiguous with zooming in to write.
