# How is access to items controlled?
Decided in #10

**Applies when:** creating an item, deciding which devices receive a change, or designing anything about sharing.

**Answer:** Through spaces. Every task, event and note lives in exactly one space, and the space decides who can see and change what's in it. Each person has a personal space, and shared spaces hold what several people do together; every member of a space can edit its items. There are no per-item rules. Quick captures are the exception until filed: they sit in the capturer's private Inbox. Ink on calendar pages follows its own rule (see "Who sees ink on calendar pages?").

**Why:** One rule per container is easier to understand and to enforce than permissions on every item.

**Rejected:** Per-item visibility, which may be useful later but adds complexity now.
